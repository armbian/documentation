    Armbian 26.11.3¶

    Released 25 November 2026

    This quarter’s release concentrates on three areas: a systematic kernel CVE backporting program, a new Hyprland desktop and first-run experience, and broad board and bootloader modernization across the Rockchip, Sunxi, and Qualcomm families.

    The most significant structural change is the armbian/cve subsystem, which introduces reviewed backport sets for the 6.1, 6.12, and 6.18 LTS kernels covering hundreds of vulnerabilities across networking, filesystems, KVM, GPU drivers, and the BPF subsystem. The workflow includes a fasttrack CI pipeline, build-test validation per series, Claude-assisted triage, and a second-review pass for shipped backports. Integration hooks in armbian/build and armbian/ci now apply these fixes automatically during image assembly, with KERNEL_CVE_PATCHES documented as a first-class build parameter.

    A second theme is the new user-facing software stack: an armbian/external repository builds and publishes third-party packages through a dedicated pipeline, feeding a Hyprland desktop port based on Omarchy, an armbian-firstrun-gui graphical wizard with on-screen keyboard and geolocation fallbacks, armbian-snapshots for Btrfs root rollback, and an update-manager GUI. The armbian-install tool gains LUKS encryption, dual-boot, UFS support, and Btrfs subvolume layouts, while desktop profiles across GNOME, KDE Plasma, XFCE, Cinnamon, and MATE receive consistent branding, HiDPI scaling, and package audits.

    Platform work spans new boards, kernel bumps, and bootloader updates: Seeed reComputer RK3576, LCKFB Taishan Pi 3M, H96 Max M9, DshanPi A1 CM5, Firefly AIBOX-K3, ArmSoM Sige6/Sige7S, Debix Model A/C, Orange Pi Zero 4, and Lenovo Legion Y700 Gen 4 join the catalog. Multiple families — bcm2711, cix-p1, imx8m, mvebu64, s5p6818, k3, sprd-ums512, and sophgo-sg200x — advance their edge kernels to 7.3, and rockchip64 bleedingedge moves to 7.4-rc. U-Boot is refreshed to v2026.10 across Orange Pi 5, NanoPi R4S, Mixtile Edge2, BPI-R4, and youyeetoo boards, with notable fixes for RK3588 SATA, SpacemiT K1 PCIe/NVMe reliability, Sunxi H616 thermal and display-engine IOMMU paths, and Rockchip camera, NPU, and HDMI pipelines.
